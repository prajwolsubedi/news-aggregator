About AI News

AI News is an automated newsletter system that curates and delivers the most important artificial intelligence news directly to your inbox every day. Our system uses AI to analyze, rank, and summarize news from multiple sources, ensuring you stay informed about the latest developments in AI without information overload.

How It Works

Our system runs a fully automated pipeline that processes news from various sources and delivers a curated newsletter to subscribers:

1. News Collection

The system automatically fetches news articles from RSS feeds and YouTube channels focused on AI and technology. This happens daily to ensure fresh content.

2. Content Processing

Videos are transcribed, and all content is analyzed using AI models to extract key information, summarize articles, and identify the most important stories.

3. Ranking & Selection

Using advanced AI ranking algorithms, the system identifies the top news stories based on relevance, importance, and timeliness. Only the most significant stories make it into the newsletter.

4. Newsletter Generation

The selected news items are compiled into a beautifully formatted email newsletter with summaries, links, and key highlights. The newsletter is designed to be informative yet concise.

5. Delivery

The newsletter is automatically sent to all active subscribers every morning, ensuring you start your day with the latest AI news.

What You Get

  • Daily curated AI news delivered to your inbox
  • Summarized articles for quick reading
  • Video transcriptions from top AI channels
  • Ranked news by importance and relevance
  • Clean, mobile-friendly email format
  • Easy unsubscribe option anytime
